What it does

Three clauses.
One pipeline.

Detect

UNet++ segmentation over 512 px tiles of a 2048 px dual-pol SAR chip, cosine-tapered into a single slick mask. Separate look-alike rejection. Reports a clean scene as a measurement, not an empty panel.

  • IoU oil 0.889 on Zenodo validation
  • Geometry area, length, width, orientation
  • Age drift-time proxy when traceable
  • EO optional Sentinel-2 corroboration

Trace

Fifty-particle RK4 backward ensemble at 5-minute steps, seeded inside the slick by rejection sampling so the cloud has the shape of the real slick. Selected at minimum ensemble variance; projected forward with an uncalibrated 95% KDE origin region.

  • Integrator RK4 backward, RK2 forward
  • Ensemble 50 particles, 0.1 m/s jitter
  • Fields cached ERA5 wind + CMEMS currents
  • Forecast percentile dispersion cone

Attribute

Historic AIS reconstructed into SQLite, resampled to one minute, filtered through a spatio-temporal funnel around the origin. Five weighted sub-scores — proximity, time, trajectory, type, behaviour — plus a track confidence term that penalises dead reckoning.

  • Funnel spatio-temporal, origin zone + window
  • Scores 5 sub-scores, weighted, with reasons
  • Confidence penalises assumed track segments
  • Result ranked likelihood, not proof
The pipeline

One button.
Twelve steps.

Every run writes a job document. Every number on the console comes from that document, which is why a finished case is linkable and exportable rather than trapped in the view.

Run a scene
  1. 01 Detect UNet++ over 512 px tiles, cosine-tapered stitch
  2. 02 Characterise Local AEQD frame, contours, PCA orientation
  3. 03 Corroborate Sentinel-2 optical chip against the SAR footprint
  4. 04 Metocean Cached ERA5 wind + CMEMS currents for the footprint
  5. 05 Hindcast RK4 backward ensemble, 50 particles, 5-min steps
  6. 06 Forecast RK2 forward ensemble, percentile dispersion cone
  7. 07 Age Hours from origin to radar pass, labelled a drift proxy
  8. 08 AIS ingest MarineCadastre schema into SQLite, resampled to 1 min
  9. 09 Filter Spatio-temporal funnel around the origin zone
  10. 10 Score Five sub-scores, weights, track confidence
  11. 11 Rank Leaderboard with reason codes, not just a score
  12. 12 Report Attribution note, HTML and PDF, from the job doc
